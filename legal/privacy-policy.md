## 2. What we collect

**Account information.** Name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) when you create an account.

**Health and fitness data.** This is a fitness app, so we collect what you log: workouts and exercise sets, body measurements, progress photos you choose to upload, meals and water intake, and any goals or preferences you set during onboarding. This is sensitive personal data in many jurisdictions (including under GDPR's "special category data" and similar state health-privacy laws) — we treat it accordingly and do not sell it.

**Payment information.** We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves. Payments are processed by Razorpay, and we receive only transaction confirmations (amount, status, a payment reference ID) — see §6.

**Coach verification documents.** If you sign up as a coach, we collect the credential and identity-verification (KYC) documents you submit for review by our team.

**Usage data.** Standard technical data — IP address, device/browser type, and app usage events — collected automatically to keep the Service secure and working.

## 3. How we use it

- To provide the Service: run your training/nutrition logs, track your progress, process subscriptions and purchases.
- To communicate with you: transactional emails (receipts, security alerts), and support responses if you contact us.
- To verify coach applicants' credentials before they can offer services on the platform.
- To keep the Service secure (fraud prevention, abuse detection) and to meet legal obligations.

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not sell personal data to third parties.
