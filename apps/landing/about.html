ABOUT · 23PrimeFit

A wellness app that's honest about what's real.

23PrimeFit is a training, nutrition, and recovery app with an AI coach grounded in your own data, plus a marketplace to book real human coaches. We're currently in a pilot, onboarding our first testers.

What we're building

One app, three pillars, a human layer on top

Training, nutrition, and recovery tracking that feeds a genuinely data-grounded AI coach — and when you want a real person, a marketplace to book a verified fitness, nutrition, yoga, or sports coach. See the full breakdown on the Features page.

Pilot stage, not a public launch
