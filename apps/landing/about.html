A wellness app that's honest about what's real.

Fynrox is a training, nutrition, and recovery app with an AI coach grounded in your own data, plus verified human professionals matched to you when you want one.

What we're building

One app, three pillars, a human layer on top

Training, nutrition, and recovery tracking that feeds a genuinely data-grounded AI coach — and when you want a real person, we match you with a verified fitness or nutrition coach. See the full breakdown on the Features page.
