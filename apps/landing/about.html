ABOUT · Fynrox

A wellness app that's honest about what's real.

Fynrox is a training, nutrition, and recovery app with an AI coach grounded in your own data, plus verified human professionals matched to you when you want one. We're currently in a pilot, onboarding our first testers.

What we're building

One app, three pillars, a human layer on top

Training, nutrition, and recovery tracking that feeds a genuinely data-grounded AI coach — and when you want a real person, we match you with a verified fitness nutrition, yoga, or sports coach. See the full breakdown on the Features page.

Where we are today

Pilot stage, not a public launch

Fynrox isn't on an app store yet. Android is a direct APK install for pilot testers; iOS access will open via TestFlight. We deliberately don't publish ratings, download counts, or user numbers on this site, because there aren't any yet — see the Download page for exactly how each platform gets the app.
