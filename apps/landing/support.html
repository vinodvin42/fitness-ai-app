FAQ

Real answers about how the app actually works

How do I log a workout?

Open a program from Train, pick a workout, and tap Start Workout. Log each set as you go, then tap Finish.

Do I have to buy programs separately?

No. Every program is included in FynroX Premium — there's no per-program purchase and no program store. See Programs.

How do I change my password?

Go to More -> Settings -> Security & Privacy -> Change Password. You'll be signed out of every session afterward, for security.

Are Reminders synced across devices?

Not yet — Reminders schedule local notifications on each device individually; there's no server-push sync between devices.

How do I export or delete my data?

Go to More -> Settings -> Security & Privacy. You can export a full copy of your data or permanently delete your account from that screen at any time — see our Privacy Policy for exactly what each does.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time from Settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, not immediately, so you keep the access you've already paid for.

More questions — price, cancellation, health data, what isn't built yet — are on the full FAQ.
