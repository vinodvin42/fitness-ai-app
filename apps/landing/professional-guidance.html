PROFESSIONAL GUIDANCE · Opt-in

A human, when the AI isn't enough.

FynroX AI answers from your data. A professional changes your plan, watches your form, and knows when to tell you to stop. Guidance is opt-in, matched rather than browsed, and every piece of your data they see is data you agreed to share.

HOW MATCHING WORKS

You ask. We match. You decide.

01

You request guidance

From inside the app: what you want help with, and for how long. Nothing is shared with anyone at this point.

02

FynroX proposes a professional

We match you to one verified professional rather than handing you a directory to pick from. In a network this small, a considered match beats a search box.

03

They accept — or the offer expires

A professional has 48 hours to accept. If they decline or let it lapse, we match you again, up to three times, and after that a person at FynroX picks it up rather than leaving you in a loop.

04

You accept, and consent is explicit

Before the relationship starts you see exactly which data they will be able to read. Nothing outside that list reaches them.

05

They work on your plan

Plan adjustments, notes and direct messages inside the app. Their view is scoped to what you shared and to the service they're approved for.

06

You end it whenever you like

Ending the relationship ends their access the same day. There is no notice period and no conversation to have first.

SCOPE

What a FynroX professional is not

Professionals on FynroX are fitness, nutrition, yoga and sports coaches. They are not doctors, physiotherapists or dietitians acting in a clinical capacity, and nothing they tell you is medical advice or a diagnosis. If something you report belongs with a clinician, the right answer from a FynroX professional is to say so — and the app itself will pause a plan rather than let it be trained through.

Every professional's credentials are verified before they can be matched to anyone, and a credential that lapses takes them out of matching until it's renewed.

WHAT THEY CAN SEE

Scoped, not total

✓

What you share

Your training log, your plan, and whichever of nutrition and recovery you chose to include. You see the list before you agree to it.

—

What they never see

Your payment details, your conversations with FynroX AI, and anything belonging to another user. Health notes you entered at onboarding are encrypted and are not part of a coaching relationship.

⏱

For how long

Only while the relationship is active. Access ends when it ends — that's enforced server-side, not by asking the professional to stop looking.
