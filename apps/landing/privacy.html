1. Who we are

2. What we collect

Account information. Name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) when you create an account.

Health and fitness data. This is a fitness app, so we collect what you log: workouts and exercise sets, body measurements, progress photos you choose to upload, meals and water intake, recovery check-ins (sleep, HRV, soreness, energy), and the goals, medical conditions, and injuries you optionally disclose during onboarding. This is sensitive personal data in many jurisdictions (including under GDPR's "special category data" and similar state health-privacy laws) — we treat it accordingly and do not sell it.

Payment information. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves. Payments are processed by Razorpay, and we receive only transaction confirmations (amount, status, a payment reference ID) — see §6.

Coach verification documents. If you sign up as a coach, we collect the credential and identity-verification (KYC) documents you submit for review by our team.

Usage data. Standard technical data — IP address, device/browser type, and app usage events — collected automatically to keep the Service secure and working.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: run your training/nutrition/recovery logs, track your progress, process subscriptions and purchases.
  • To communicate with you: transactional emails (receipts, security alerts) and support responses if you contact us.
  • To verify coach applicants' credentials before they can offer services on the platform.
  • To keep the Service secure (fraud prevention, abuse detection) and to meet legal obligations.

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Your consent choices

Inside the app's Privacy Settings, you can grant or withdraw consent separately for three real, distinct purposes:

  • Marketing emails — promotional/product emails, separate from transactional emails (receipts, security alerts) you'll always receive.
  • Data analytics — using your usage data to understand and improve the product.
  • Health data processing — using the health/fitness data described in §2 to power features like the AI coach.

Each of these is tracked individually and can be changed at any time; a consent that's never been explicitly decided is treated as "not yet decided," not as silently granted or declined.

5. Your rights: access, export, and deletion

Access and export. You can request a full export of your data from the app's Settings → Security screen at any time. This is a real, self-service feature — it generates a structured export of your onboarding profile, workout sessions and set logs, meal logs, body measurements, program purchases, subscriptions, and reminders directly from your live account data, on demand.

Deletion. You can permanently delete your account and its data from the same screen, after confirming your password. This is irreversible.
