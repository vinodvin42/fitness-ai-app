QUESTIONS

Fynrox Active 50+: common questions

Is it safe to start after 50?

Active 50+ focuses on mobility, balance and joint-friendly strength. Check with your doctor before starting if you have a health condition.

How long is the programme and what does it cost?

It runs for 12 weeks and costs ₹4,999 including GST. It's bought separately from your Basic or Prime plan.

Can I follow it at home?

Yes. Choose home workouts and the plan uses only the equipment you have. You don't need a gym membership.

Do you provide Indian diet guidance?

Yes. Nutrition guidance is built around Indian food, including vegetarian options.

How do I start?

Download the Fynrox app, choose your goal, then pick this programme and pay securely inside the app.
