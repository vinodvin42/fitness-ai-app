How it works

Reach out, get set up, share your link

1

Reach out

Creator partnerships are set up by our team today, not a self-serve signup form — tell us your name, platform, and handle through the Support page.

2

Get your code

Once set up, you get a real, unique campaign link/code tied to your profile, plus an optional discount code your audience can use at checkout.

3

Track referrals

From the Creator Partner Portal, see the activity your link/code has driven — clicks, registrations, and conversions — attributed through the app's real acquisition-tracking model.

Commercial terms

Commission-based, per creator

Each creator partner has an admin-configurable commission percentage, defaulting to 20%.

Honest note on payouts: commission percentages are recorded on your creator profile, but payouts today are entered manually by our team for an agreed period rather than auto-calculated from live attributed revenue — there's no fully-automated "your link drove ₹X, here's Y% of it" calculation running yet. We'd rather tell you that plainly than promise a number the system doesn't compute for you automatically today.
