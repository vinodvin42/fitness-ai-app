GYM PARTNER PROGRAM · Application-based

Bring FynroX to your members.

Partner your gym with FynroX: your members get the app's full training, nutrition, recovery, and AI coaching tools, and you get a real invite code and a simple activity dashboard — no POS, payroll, or membership management, just app onboarding.

How it works

Application, approval, invite code

1

Apply

Tell us about your gym — name and contact details. Your application starts in the real "application" status while our team reviews it.

2

Get approved

Once approved, your gym profile is activated and you're issued a unique, server-generated invite code your members use when they sign up in the app.

3

Track activity

From the Gym Partner Portal, see aggregate member counts — how many members signed up, completed onboarding, and logged a first workout. No individual member health, nutrition, photo, or AI-conversation data is ever shown to a gym partner.

Commercial terms

Commission-based, per gym

Each partner gym has an admin-configurable commission percentage, defaulting to 15%. You can have multiple locations under one gym partner account, each with its own equipment/description notes.

Honest note on gym-attributed revenue: a real, structured revenue-sharing mechanism for gym-attributed subscriptions hasn't been built yet — the commission percentage exists on your gym profile today, but there's no live payout calculation running against it until that mechanism ships. We won't promise a payout number that doesn't have real math behind it yet.
