FAQ · Straight answers

Questions people actually ask.

Including the ones with an unflattering answer. If something isn't built, this page says it isn't built.

Is FynroX available to download?

Not yet. It's in a closed pilot with a small number of testers, so there's no App Store or Google Play listing to link to. Join the early access list and we'll email you when there's a place.

What does it cost?

There is one paid tier, FynroX Premium, and everything in the app is in it — programs included, with no per-program purchase. Current prices are on the pricing page, shown inclusive of GST.

Is there a free trial?

No. We'd rather charge from day one and make cancelling easy than run a trial that converts people who forgot about it.

How do I cancel?

From inside the app, in two taps, with no phone call and no retention flow. Your access runs to the end of the period you've already paid for.

Do I need a gym?

No. Onboarding asks what equipment you can actually reach, and every exercise you're shown is one that profile allows. The same program runs in a commercial gym, at home, or in a hotel room — with different exercises, not a shorter version.

Is the AI coach a substitute for a doctor?

No, and it will tell you so. FynroX AI gives general fitness and wellness guidance grounded in your logged data. It doesn't diagnose, interpret symptoms, or tell you whether a pain is safe to train through. See what an AI coach can and can't do.

Can I work with a real coach?

Yes, and it's opt-in. You request guidance, FynroX matches you with one verified professional, and you see exactly what data they'll be able to read before you accept. How guidance works.

Can I choose my own coach from a list?

Not today. In a network this size a considered match beats a directory, so FynroX proposes one professional at a time. If they decline or don't respond within 48 hours we match you again, up to three times, and after that a person at FynroX picks it up.

What happens to my health information?

The health notes you enter at onboarding — conditions and injuries — are encrypted at rest and are not shared with a professional as part of a coaching relationship. You can request an export or a deletion from inside the app and watch its status. Safety & privacy.

Will FynroX ever stop me training?

Yes, deliberately. If your health screen or a check-in raises something that shouldn't be trained through, you'll either see a warning before the session or the plan is paused until you've spoken to a professional. A declared heart condition always pauses it.

Does it sync with my watch or ring?

Not yet. Recovery metrics — resting heart rate, sleep, HRV, soreness, energy — are self-reported today. Device sync isn't built, and we'd rather say that than list it as a feature.

Can I scan barcodes when logging food?

Barcode lookup is built against an open food database, but the catalogue is uneven outside common packaged products. When a scan finds nothing, the app says so rather than guessing at the numbers.

My gym gave me a code. What do I do with it?

Open the invite link they sent you, or enter the code in the app during signup. It links your account to the gym so your plan is built around their equipment, and credits them for the introduction.

Is my data sold or shared with partners?

No. Gym and creator partners see aggregate counts about their own programme — how many people they introduced, how many activated — never an individual member's training, nutrition or health data.

I'm a coach. How do I join?

Through the professional application form. We verify certifications before anyone is matched to you, so list what you can evidence.
