What joining through your gym changes

Your plan matches their floor

Your training plan is built around the equipment your gym actually has, so you're not handed a programme for a machine that isn't there.

Your gym sees counts, not you

They can see how many members joined and how many are active. They never see your name, your training, your food, your photos, or anything about your health.

It stays your account

If you leave the gym, your account, your history and your data come with you. Nothing is deleted and nothing transfers to them.
