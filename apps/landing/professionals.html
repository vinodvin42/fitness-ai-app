FOR PROFESSIONALS · Application-based

Coach people whose data you can actually see.

You don't build a profile and wait. FynroX matches you with clients who have asked for guidance, and you start with their training log, their plan and their recovery trend already in front of you.

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

No marketplace, no bidding

01

Clients are matched to you

There is no public directory, no ranking and nothing to pay for placement in. A client asks for guidance, we match one professional, and you accept or decline within 48 hours.

02

You start with their data

Sets, reps and RPE they logged themselves; their nutrition and recovery if they shared it. Scoped to what the client consented to, and it ends when the relationship does.

03

Credentials are verified

Yours and everyone else's. It's the reason a client can be matched rather than asked to vet a stranger, and it's why an expired certification takes you out of matching until it's renewed.

04

Earnings you can see

Your professional app shows what you've earned per client relationship and where each amount is in its payout cycle — not a number that appears in your bank account with no explanation.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Three things worth knowing

The pilot is small. We're onboarding a limited number of professionals alongside a limited number of users, so an application may sit for a while before there is a client to match you with. We'd rather say that than imply a waiting list of people.

Your scope is coaching, not clinical care. If a client reports something that belongs with a clinician, escalating it is the expected behaviour, not a failure. The app backs you up: it pauses a plan itself when a health screen says it should.

Commercial terms are still being finalised. Rates and the payout schedule are agreed individually during onboarding and are not published here, because publishing a number we might change would be worse than publishing none.

APPLY

Tell us what you do

A real person reads every application. We'll come back to you by email, including when the answer is no.

Strength coaching, nutrition, yoga, sports conditioning — your words, not a dropdown.

Who you've worked with, how long, and which certifications you hold. We verify certifications before anyone is matched to you, so list what you can evidence.
