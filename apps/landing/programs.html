UNDERNEATH

The exercise library

Every program is assembled from one library, with step-by-step instructions, muscle group and equipment filters, and a substitute for each movement. The substitute is the part that matters: it's what lets the same program run in a commercial gym, a hotel room and a garage without becoming three different programs.

Not built yet: video demonstrations. Every exercise has written instructions and images; none of them have video, and we'd rather say so than imply otherwise.
