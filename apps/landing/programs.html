PROGRAMS · Included in Premium

Programs that fit the gym you actually train in.

Three structured programs, a full exercise library behind them, and an equipment profile that decides which exercises you're ever shown. No program here assumes a machine you don't have.

THE THREE

Pick the one that matches your goal

FL

Fat loss

Training that keeps the strength you have while you're in a deficit, paired with nutrition logging in Fuel so the deficit is something you can see rather than something you assume.

MG

Muscle gain

Progressive volume across the week, with the sets, reps and RPE you log feeding the next week's targets. Personal records are tracked per exercise, not per session.

ST

Strength

Heavier, lower-rep work on the main lifts with accessories chosen from what your equipment profile allows. If the barbell isn't there, the program doesn't pretend it is.

UNDERNEATH

The exercise library

Every program is assembled from one library, with step-by-step instructions, muscle group and equipment filters, and a substitute for each movement. The substitute is the part that matters: it's what lets the same program run in a commercial gym, a hotel room and a garage without becoming three different programs.

Not built yet: video demonstrations. Every exercise has written instructions and images; none of them have video, and we'd rather say so than imply otherwise.

WHAT THEY COST

Nothing, separately.

Programs are included in FynroX Premium. There is no per-program purchase, no program store and no upsell inside a plan you're already following. See pricing.
