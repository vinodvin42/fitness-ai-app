What's actually in the app

Six real pieces, working together

No filler modules — every card below describes functionality that's genuinely in the current pilot build, not a roadmap slide.

Structured training, built to progress

Follow real training programs backed by a full exercise library — step-by-step instructions, muscle-group and equipment filters, and smart alternatives. Every set you log (weight, reps, RPE) feeds your workout history and personal records.

Nutrition you'll actually keep logging

Log meals, browse recipes, and track water intake in Fuel. A monthly nutrition calendar shows how consistent you've really been — not just today's numbers.

Recovery, tracked honestly

Check in daily on resting heart rate, sleep, HRV, soreness, and energy, and see how they trend over the last 30 days — real, self-reported signal, not a guess.

An AI coach that knows your data

Chat with 23Prime AI from Train, Fuel, or Recover — grounded in your real workouts, nutrition, and progress, not a generic chatbot.

General fitness and wellness guidance only — never a substitute for professional medical advice.

A real coach, when you want one

Browse verified fitness, nutrition, yoga, and sports coaches, book a session, and message them directly once you're paired — a human layer on top of the AI, not instead of it.

Progress you can actually see

Personal records, progress photos, a weight-trend chart, and streaks across training, nutrition, and hydration — plus a Timeline tying every logged workout, meal, and milestone together.
