What's actually in the app

Three of six real pieces, working together

Structured training, built to progress

Follow real training programs backed by a full exercise library — step-by-step instructions, muscle-group and equipment filters, and smart alternatives. Every set you log (weight, reps, RPE) feeds your workout history and personal records.

An AI coach that knows your data
