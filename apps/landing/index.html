Privacy

Who sees your data

What coaches and gyms can see, and what you control.

  • Only your current coach

    Only a coach you're actively working with can see your data. Access stops when they or Fynrox end or complete the coaching, though their own notes stay with them.

  • Health data needs your consent

    Without health-data consent, your coach sees your goals, training level, diet type and session history, plus messages and anything else you send them. With it, they also see your recent workouts, check-ins and body measurements.

  • You choose the rest, per coach

    With health-data consent on, food logs are shared by default, and daily steps and sleep and recovery stay off unless you turn them on. Progress photos and AI chats aren't shared with coaches.

  • Gyms see member counts

    A gym sees member counts, not your logs.

  • Change it or take a copy

    Turn health-data consent off, or request a copy of your data, in the app's Data & Privacy screen. Progress photos and AI chats aren't in the copy.
