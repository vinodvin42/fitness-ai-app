Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Fynrox suitable for beginners?

Yes. Your plan starts from your current level and builds up gradually.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Every programme has home and gym options.

Can I follow the plan at home?

Yes. Choose home workouts and the plan uses only the equipment you have.

Do you provide Indian diet guidance?

Yes. Nutrition guidance is built around Indian food, including vegetarian options.

Is the programme suitable for people over 50?

Yes. Fynrox Active 50+ focuses on mobility, balance and joint-friendly strength. Check with your doctor before starting if you have a health condition.

Can women with PCOS join?

Yes. Fynrox Women's Wellness supports PCOS-friendly habits. It's general fitness guidance, so follow your doctor's advice alongside it.

How do I download the Fynrox app?

Tap "Download App" on this page, then install Fynrox on your Android phone.

How do I purchase a plan?

Download the app, choose your goal, then pick a plan or programme and pay securely inside the app.

Can I cancel or upgrade my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade any time in the app. Cancelling takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
