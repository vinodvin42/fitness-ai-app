# Open Questions & Gaps — Coach App

Read alongside [../09-open-questions-gaps.md](../admin/07-open-questions-gaps.md) (admin) and [../mobile/07-open-questions-gaps.md](../mobile/07-open-questions-gaps.md) (consumer app) — the recurring gaps (no auth flows fully specified where relevant, no empty/loading/error states, no published design tokens, iOS-only) apply here too and aren't repeated below.

## 1. Coach discovery/booking is designed twice, inconsistently — highest-priority item across all three files
Full comparison in [06-cross-app-integration.md](06-cross-app-integration.md) §2. This is the single most consequential inconsistency found across the whole project so far: two different screen flows, two different coach-category taxonomies (one of which — Yoga/Sports in the consumer app — doesn't exist anywhere else in the product), and two different booking flow shapes. **This needs a product/design decision before any backend or frontend work on coaching discovery/booking starts**, or engineering will end up building against whichever version it happens to see first, and rework will follow.

## 2. Bottom-nav labels have now drifted three ways
- Consumer app (`v1-user`): Today, Train, Fuel, Recover, More.
- Coach app (`v1-coach`, coach-side): Dashboard, Clients, Calendar, Messages, More.
- Coach app (`v1-coach`, `user-my-professional-team` screen): Home, Explore, Sessions, Messages, Profile.
The first two are arguably fine — they're two different apps for two different roles and can reasonably have different tab sets. The third is the problem: it's a *user-facing* screen (part of the consumer journey) that doesn't match the consumer app's own established tab bar. **Recommend:** fix `user-my-professional-team` (and any other `user-`-prefixed screens in this file) to use the consumer app's actual tab bar before implementation, and treat this as a signal to double-check the rest of this file's `user-` screens for the same drift.

## 3. Three different default accent colors across three apps
Admin = mint green, consumer app = blue, coach app = lime/yellow-green (see [04-design-system.md](04-design-system.md) §1). Given the consumer app also supports a *user-selectable* accent color, one plausible resolution: pick one brand-default accent (mint green, since it's used in the admin console and is closest to "PrimeFit green" implied by the leaf/nutrition iconography across all three files) and treat blue and lime as either legacy/inconsistent choices to fix, or intentional per-role defaults to keep. Needs a design decision, not an engineering guess — this blocks defining the shared `packages/ui` token set in [../07-project-structure.md](../platform/project-structure.md).

## 4. Calendar, Messages, and More tabs have zero screens
Only Dashboard and one Clients destination were actually designed for the coach app's own shell. Messages is a particularly notable gap given the consumer app has a fully designed `user-coach-messaging` screen (with shared plan/video cards) — the coach's side of that same conversation doesn't exist yet anywhere. **Action needed:** these need a design pass before they can be scoped for the roadmap.

**25 Aug 2026 — re-checked, still genuinely open.** Re-reviewed while working through the coach app's remaining gaps after Discovery & Booking shipped (gap §1). Unlike gap §1, this one has no unblocked half to build ahead of the decision: Calendar has no spec for what a coach's calendar should even show (an editable availability grid? a read-only upcoming-sessions list? something else — a real `Booking` entity exists as of 25 Aug 2026, so data isn't the blocker, a UI/UX decision is); Messages needs both a design AND a `Message` data model/delivery mechanism that doesn't exist anywhere in this build yet, on either app; More has zero frames to build against at all, on either app or role, unlike apps/user-mobile's own More tab (§N), which was designed. `apps/coach-mobile/src/navigation/MainTabs.tsx` continues to render all three as real, honest "Coming soon" screens rather than inventing layouts with no design source — the same discipline this build has held to for every other undesigned screen (Body Composition, Recovery & Devices, live chat support, and others).

## 5. No coach-side earnings/payout visibility
The admin console tracks Coach Settlements (10.06) with a Hold/Approve workflow, but no screen in this file lets a coach see their own upcoming or past payouts, session earnings breakdown, or settlement status. This is a reasonable early ask from real coaches (self-service earnings visibility) and is currently a complete gap between what admin manages and what a coach can see about their own money.

## 6. "Create Workout Plan" / "Create Nutrition Plan" have no destination
Both dashboard variants surface these as primary quick actions, but no content-authoring screen exists in this file (and the consumer-app content — Programs/Exercises/Recipes — is admin-authored per [../mobile/06-cross-app-integration.md](../mobile/06-cross-app-integration.md) §1, not obviously coach-authored). **Decision needed:** can individual coaches author their own workout/nutrition plans for their clients (distinct from the admin-managed content library), or do these buttons route into assigning existing admin-approved programs? This changes both the coach app's scope and the Programs data model in [../06-data-model.md](../admin/06-data-model.md).

## 7. KYC (Aadhaar) collection needs explicit privacy handling
The credential-verification screen collects a government ID (Aadhaar, India's national ID) directly in an onboarding upload flow. This is highly sensitive PII and should be handled with explicit, scoped consent and secure storage/redaction practices — worth an explicit security/privacy review before this screen is implemented, separate from the general privacy gaps already noted for the consumer app's onboarding ([../mobile/07-open-questions-gaps.md](../mobile/07-open-questions-gaps.md) §5).

## 20 Aug 2026 — Phase 5 started: decisions made to unblock `apps/coach-mobile`, not a full resolution

Gap §1 explicitly says this needs a product/design decision, not an engineering guess — that's still true for the *consumer-facing* discovery/booking screens themselves, which are NOT built this pass (see below). But leaving the underlying data model undecided was blocking even the parts of Phase 5 that don't depend on it (coach auth, onboarding, verification, a coach's own dashboard), so a scoped default was made to unblock those:

- **Gap §1 (discovery/booking designed twice):** decided — per this doc's own recommendation in [06-cross-app-integration.md](06-cross-app-integration.md) §2, `v1-coach`'s version is treated as the authoritative shape for the **backend data model**: the Fitness Coach / Nutrition Professional / Fitness + Nutrition taxonomy (not the consumer app's Yoga/Sports categories, which don't correspond to anything a coach can register as anywhere else in the product and are treated as stale), and `Relationship` as the parent object with `Booking`/session as a child (per [05-data-model.md](05-data-model.md) §3). **This does NOT mean the actual discovery/booking UI screens are built** — neither version's screens (the consumer app's 4-screen flow or this file's 5-screen flow) were implemented this pass. What's real: `apps/api`'s `Professional`/`ProfessionalCredential`/`Relationship` models and `apps/coach-mobile`'s auth/onboarding/dashboard, none of which required picking a discovery/booking screen design to build. The actual "find and book a coach" experience — on either app — is still open, and still genuinely needs a product/design call given how consequential gap §1 correctly says this is.
- **Gap §2 (bottom-nav drift):** decided for the coach-side app — `apps/coach-mobile` uses its own real Dashboard/Clients/Calendar/Messages/More tab bar throughout, including anywhere a discovery/booking screen eventually gets built inside it. The stray `Home/Explore/Sessions/Messages/Profile` set on the old `user-my-professional-team` frame was not carried forward. The consumer app's own tab bar (Today/Train/Fuel/Recover/More) is unaffected — no discovery/booking screen was added there this pass.
- **Gap §3 (three different accent colors):** NOT resolved, deliberately — `apps/coach-mobile` uses its own designed lime/yellow-green accent (see `apps/coach-mobile/src/theme/tokens.ts`), same precedent as `apps/admin-web` keeping its own mint and `apps/user-mobile` keeping its own blue: each app ships what was actually designed for it rather than an engineering-guessed unification. Whether the three should converge on one brand accent is still a real, open product/design decision — see [04-design-system.md](04-design-system.md) §1.
- **Gap §6 ("Create Workout Plan"/"Create Nutrition Plan" have no destination):** NOT resolved — `apps/coach-mobile`'s Dashboard renders these as real-looking but inert quick actions (same "Soon" precedent as `apps/admin-web`'s sidebar), since whether coaches can author their own plans (vs. only assigning admin-approved Programs) is a real scope decision this pass can't make.
- **Gap §5 (no coach-side earnings/payout visibility) and gap §4 (Calendar/Messages/More have zero designed screens):** NOT resolved — `apps/coach-mobile`'s Calendar/Messages/More tabs are inert placeholders, same as gap §6.
- **Gap §7 (KYC/Aadhaar privacy handling):** NOT resolved — the KYC upload in `apps/coach-mobile`'s onboarding is real (a professional can submit a document), but it's stored with the exact same mechanism as every other credential document in this build (base64-in-Postgres, no encryption-at-rest beyond whatever the database itself provides, no redaction, no scoped-access control) — see `apps/api/prisma/schema.prisma`'s `Professional.kycDocumentData` comment. This is explicitly flagged, not silently shipped as if it were handled — a real security/privacy review is still needed before this should ever hold a real government ID.

Gaps §1 (the actual discovery/booking screens), §4, §5, §6, §7 remain genuinely open in the sense the original gap describes, even though a scoped decision was made to keep other Phase 5 work moving.

## 25 Aug 2026 — Gap §1 resolved: the actual discovery/booking screens are now built

Gap §1's remaining half — the actual "find and book a coach" screens, still open as of the 20 Aug 2026 update above — required a real product/design decision, not an engineering guess, exactly as the original gap said. That decision was put to the product owner directly (which flow/taxonomy should be authoritative for the *screens*, not just the backend data model already decided 20 Aug 2026), with four options laid out. **Decision: adopt `v1-coach`'s version** — its fuller 7-screen flow (Discovery Filters, Discovery List, Coach Profile Detail, Booking: Service Selection, Booking Confirmation, My Professional Team, Change Professional) and its Fitness/Nutrition/Fitness+Nutrition taxonomy — as authoritative for both apps, matching this doc's own 20 Aug 2026 recommendation and keeping the screens consistent with the data model decision already made.

What shipped, built against that decision:
- **Backend:** a new `coaching` module (`apps/api/src/modules/coaching`) with `ProfessionalServiceOffering` (seeded, not yet coach-authored — no screen in either Figma file lets a coach set their own rates), `Booking`, and `RelationshipChangeRequest` Prisma models; six endpoints (discover, profile detail, availability, create booking, list my team, submit change request); confirming a booking upserts a real `Relationship` row, closing the parent/child model gap §5's own doc describes.
- **Frontend:** all six screens live in `apps/user-mobile` (not `apps/coach-mobile`) — these are the `user-`-prefixed screens in the Figma naming; a coach doesn't need a discovery flow to find themselves. Discovery Filters and Discovery List were combined into one screen, same "combine near-duplicate design screens" precedent already used elsewhere in this build.
- **Deliberate simplifications, flagged rather than silently shipped:** no rating/review system (no `Review` entity exists anywhere in this build — Discovery/Profile honestly omit rating instead of fabricating it); a fixed 9:00–18:00 UTC-based availability grid with real conflict-checking against existing bookings, not a coach-configured schedule (no screen designs one); no payment collection at booking time — bookings confirm immediately with a real, honestly-displayed `priceCents`, matching how Subscribe/Program Purchase originally shipped before Razorpay was wired in as a separate pass; wiring Booking through the existing Razorpay rails is a natural, scoped follow-up, not done this pass.

Gap §1 is now fully resolved — both the data-model half (20 Aug 2026) and the screens half (25 Aug 2026). Gaps §4, §5, §6, §7 remain open as described above.

## 26 Aug 2026 — Gap §4 partially resolved: Calendar is real

Calendar's UI/UX question from the 25 Aug 2026 update above — "an editable availability grid? a read-only upcoming-sessions list? something else" — was put to a scoping decision rather than left open: a real, sectioned list of the coach's own `Booking` rows (Upcoming grouped by day, Past capped at the most recent 50 with an honest truncation note), not an editable-availability-grid or an invented month-grid calendar widget. This is deliberately smaller than either of those richer possibilities — same "honest smaller scope than an unspec'd feature" precedent as 09.01's compare toggle in the admin console — but it's real: `GET /professionals/me/schedule` (`apps/api/src/modules/coaching/coaching.service.ts`'s `listMySchedule`) backs `apps/coach-mobile`'s Calendar tab, no longer a "Coming soon" placeholder. The Coach Dashboard's Sessions This Week and Today's Schedule stats (flagged not-real in the 20 Aug 2026 update) are also real now, built on the same Booking data.

**Messages and More remain genuinely open** — Messages still needs both a design and a real-time delivery mechanism decision (polling vs. WebSocket vs. a vendor), and still has no `Message` data model anywhere in this build; More still has zero frames to build against, on either app or role. Gap §4 is now "Calendar resolved, Messages and More still open," not fully closed.
