01

Consumer app (user-mobile) — remaining gaps

Everything else in the 5-phase mobile build is real. Two items remain, both genuinely outside engineering's control.

Phase 2Recovery & Devices
needs hardware
Missing
Wearable/BLE pairing, HealthKit/Google Fit sync, the Readiness Score card, and any recovery-trend chart. No Bluetooth or health-platform integration exists anywhere in this build.
Why stuck
Needs a real device to pair against and an Apple/Google developer account for HealthKit/Google Fit entitlements — there's no honest way to fake this with mock data that would still prove the integration works.
Next step
Get an Apple Developer and/or Google Play Console account, plus a physical device with a wearable to test pairing against. Nothing to plan beyond that — this is acquisition, not a design question.
Phase 4Live chat support
needs a vendor or staff
Missing
Real-time chat with a support agent, from Settings → Support.
Why stuck
Needs either real human agents on the other end, or a support-platform account (Intercom/Zendesk-style). Building an AI-powered chat that presented itself as "live chat" would misrepresent what it is.
Next step
Decide staffing model first (in-house agents vs. a vendor), then create that vendor's account if applicable. Support Tickets (admin 08.01) already covers async support in the meantime.

Coach app (coach-mobile) — remaining gaps

Auth, onboarding, dashboard, discovery/booking, and — as of 26 Aug 2026 — Calendar are all real. Two tabs remain with no design source at all.

Messages / More tabs
needs a design decision
Missing
Both tabs render honest "Coming soon" placeholders. Messages needs both a design and a message data model that exists nowhere in this build; More has zero frames anywhere, on either app or role. (Calendar, previously listed here alongside these two, shipped 26 Aug 2026 as a real sectioned Booking list — see the Coach app README.)

Admin 02 — Users

Directory, Profile, and the Sensitive Data Access Request workflow are all real. 26 Aug 2026: Bulk-select + suspend/reactivate shipped too — re-investigated directly against the schema rather than trusting this section's own prior framing, and found it didn't actually need a product decision: it's the exact same account-operations pattern already shipped for Professional/AdminUser, not the trust-and-safety moderation call 08.04 Safety/Abuse Reports still needs. User gained a real status field; the Directory gained a real checkbox column and bulk-action bar (one real suspend/reactivate call per selected row). One spec'd primary action remains.

Auto-renew toggle
needs your decision
Missing
A toggle on the User Profile's Subscription card. Subscription has no autoRenew column, and no billing/renewal engine exists to make a real toggle meaningful even if the field existed.
Why stuck
This is bigger than a field — it implies a scheduled renewal job (charge the saved payment method on the billing date) that doesn't exist anywhere in this build. Building the field without the engine would be a switch that does nothing.
Next step
Decide whether subscriptions should auto-renew at all in v1, or stay manual-renewal-only. If yes, this is really a Commerce/billing-engine project, not a one-screen admin feature.

Create/edit/publish for Programs, Exercises, and Recipes, plus the Review/Approval queue, are all real. One content type remains unmodeled.

05.04 Educational Content
needs your decision
Missing
An entirely new content type — no EducationalContent entity, no admin screen, no mobile surface to read it.
Why stuck
Not fundamentally infra-blocked like Recovery & Devices, but genuinely two stacked decisions, not one — re-investigated 26 Aug 2026 and this line's earlier "one small decision" framing turned out to be wrong. Even granting a content-taxonomy call (articles? video? both?), there's still no mobile IA slot to attach it to anywhere in user-mobile's navigation — unlike Calendar or Geographic below, which both had a clear, already-designed destination waiting, this would need an invented nav placement with zero design precedent in either Figma file.

Admin 06 — Commerce

Payments directory/detail and Plans CRUD are real. Re-investigated this session — the remaining gaps are genuine, not stale, and each is a money-policy question before it's an engineering one.

06.01 Subscriptions revenue waterfall
needs your decision
Missing
A Gross → Discounts → Gateway fees → Refunds → Settlements → Net chart. 3 of 6 stages have no backing entity.
Why stuck
Rendering a waterfall with half its stages fabricated would misrepresent real revenue — worse than an honest gap. Genuinely blocked on 06.04/06.05 below existing first.
Next step
Build after Refunds and Coupons below exist for real — this becomes a straightforward aggregation once they do.
06.04 Refunds
needs your decision
Missing
A Refund entity, a refund-request flow (mobile or admin-initiated), and a real Razorpay refund-API call.
Why stuck
A refund is a real money-movement event — marking a Payment "refunded" internally without an actual gateway call would misrepresent a financial record. Needs a refund policy first: who can request one, what's the approval flow, what's the eligibility window.
Next step
Decide the refund policy, then wire razorpayClient.ts's missing refund-API wrapper against it — same "real action or nothing" bar every other financial screen in this build already holds to.
06.05 Coupons
needs your decision
Missing
A Coupon entity and a discount-code field anywhere in the checkout flow. Nothing to apply one against exists today.
Why stuck
Needs a discount policy — percentage vs. flat, single-use vs. reusable, expiry rules — before the entity shape can be designed.
Next step
Decide discount structure, then this follows the same real-CRUD pattern adminPlans already established for SubscriptionPlan.

Admin 07 — Growth

07.01/07.02 Influencers
needs your decision
Missing

07.04 Campaigns & Attribution
needs your decision
Missing
A Campaign entity, an attribution model, and any UTM/campaign-tracking mechanism.
Why stuck
Needs a decision on which attribution model to support (last-touch is the simplest) before the tracking mechanism can be designed.
Next step
Lower priority than Influencers above — revisit once marketing actually runs campaigns worth attributing.

Admin 08 — Support & Safety

Support Tickets and (this session) Escalations are real. Two items remain, each needing its own filing flow.

08.03 Complaints
needs your decision
Missing
A formal complaint-filing flow (neither mobile app has one) and a Complaint entity with a "dossier" of facts.
Why stuck
Unlike Escalations, there's no existing action a "resolution" could safely reuse, and it's unclear who a complaint is even against — a User? a Professional? Both are plausible.
Next step
Decide who can be complained about and what a resolution actually does. Once scoped, the shape mirrors Escalations closely — a workflow record with a real filing action and a real resolve action.
08.04 Safety / Abuse Reports
needs your decision
Missing
An abuse-report flow, a SafetyReport entity, and any real moderation action to back it.
Why stuck
A safety queue with no real moderation action behind it would misrepresent trust & safety capability this build doesn't have — a sharper version of the reasoning that kept Refunds and Complaints unbuilt too. 26 Aug 2026: User did gain a real status field, but for Module 02.01's account-operations use (suspend/reactivate, no reason code, no appeal) — a genuinely narrower thing than what this item needs (ban/appeal/severity, who can flip it and on what grounds). Still needs a real moderation-policy decision, not just a schema field.
Next step
Decide moderation capability: does a report actually lead to a ban/restriction, and who can impose one? Module 02.01's status field and suspend/reactivate endpoint shape could be reused as the mechanism once that's decided — the schema follow-up would be small.

Admin 10 — Finance

Its real blocker — one ledger or two — was decided 26 Aug 2026: one ledger. Finance is a deeper reporting/operations layer over the same Payment/Subscription data Commerce already owns, extended with 3 new entities (Expense, Invoice, TaxConfig), not a parallel bookkeeping system — full reasoning and schema in reports/finance-architecture-plan.html. That decision has since shipped: 7 of the 10 Finance screens are real — 10.01 Dashboard, 10.02 Revenue, 10.03's Expenses half, 10.04 Invoices, 10.05's Receivables half, 10.08 Taxes & Compliance (a real admin-entered jurisdiction/rate settings screen, not an automated tax-rule engine), and 10.10 Reports — see adminFinance.service.ts's own doc comment for the full endpoint-by-endpoint breakdown.
